HERO SELECTION AND HYDRATION

Choose a hero before the first move in any room. Selection locks once play
starts; Retry resets the current attempt and lets you choose again. Entering
another room also unlocks selection. Progress from earlier rooms is retained.

Water gives the room's base duration multiplied by the selected hero's shield
multiplier. For example, room 1 gives Li+ 12 seconds, Na+ 9.6 seconds, or K+
8 seconds. A pickup renews the shield; it does not stack unlimited time.
Speed values, durations, and all multipliers are gameplay parameters.
Individual drops start slightly above the listed base speed and accelerate.
Rain starts with movement. Walls intercept it; shields block toxic hits.

RULES AND CONTROLS

Arrows / WASD: move. Hold a point on the canvas: steer toward it.
Space / Burst: dash, with a four-second recharge.
P / Pause: pause. R / Retry: retry this room. Touch controls are included.
The page pauses when backgrounded. Sound is off until enabled. Decorative
motion is reduced when the browser requests reduced motion.

Collect water: +50 points and a temporary shield. Shields reduce trap pull,
prevent trap damage, and allow crossing active barriers. They do not prevent
wrong-protein penalties or ignition-core deaths.
Wrong protein: red rings, broken-bond sparks, knockback, -40 points, -1 life.
Correct protein: hold still in its marked green pocket. It gives +300, a green
celebration, +2 per whole second left, and +100 for a damage-free rescue.
A brief celebration appears before the score dialog and next-room button.
Docking resets if you leave the pocket or move too quickly.
Orange ignition cores: -80, flames, and Game Over even with a water shield.
No lives or no time: Game Over / Try it later! Retry restarts this room.
The fifth rescue completes the adventure. Play again starts at room 1.
Best scores use a new localStorage key so earlier three-room scores do not mix.

CHEMISTRY NOTES

Aqueous Li+, Na+ and K+ are colourless. The characters' screen colours are
artistic approximations of the elements' familiar flame-test colours, not
exact reproductions of emission spectra. The toxic characters' palettes are
fictional; they are not proposed aqueous-ion colours or spectral measurements.

The hydration trend Li+ > Na+ > K+ informs the relative shield durations.
Water oxygen points toward the positive ion. The number of drawn waters and
rings is illustrative and does not assert a particular coordination number.
Faster movement, temporary shielding, attractive traps and ignition cores are
arcade mechanics, not quantitative molecular-dynamics or binding predictions.
Arsenic is a metalloid; As* denotes arsenic-containing species. Dissolved
alkali cations are not alkali metal. Water does not make toxic metals safe.

VALIDATION

Automated movement tests completed all five rooms with each of the three
heroes (15 combinations), within the 60-second limit. Every hero passed the
first cage by holding right and collecting water, without taking damage.
Checks also exercised wrong-protein recoil, fatal ignition through a shield,
paused time, retry, selection locks, keyboard and pointer control, the delayed
win dialog, next-room reset, and disabled/configured play notification logic.
All five room backgrounds and all three portraits were rendered and visually
inspected. The GIF shows accelerated gameplay from those same game functions.
A full browser layout or real-device touch test was not available.
No files have been pushed or deployed to GitHub by the assistant.
