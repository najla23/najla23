NEW IN CHEMISTRY STORM

Four labelled toxic hazards fall from the first move: Pb2+, Hg2+, Cd2+,
and As* (arsenic-containing species). Eight initial drops are visible before
movement; nothing harmful moves until you start. New drops have a dashed
warning lane before entering. Rain is faster and more frequent in later rooms.
Compared with the previous Chemistry Storm version, initial falling speeds,
acceleration, and speed caps are tripled; the spawn rate is multiplied by six.
Spawn intervals are 0.1467, 0.12, and 0.0933 seconds in rooms 1, 2, and 3.
New drops start arriving 0.15 seconds after movement begins.
Walls intercept rain. A temporary water shield blocks a toxic hit; otherwise
it costs 40 points and one life. Drops disappear after impact.

Two orange ignition cores are present in every room. Touching one costs
80 points and ends the attempt, even with a water shield. A short flame
animation plays before GAME OVER / "Up in flames." / "Try it later!"
This is a fictional arcade reaction, not an actual K+ reaction.

THE GAME

You control a potassium ion (K+). It starts trapped and must reach the green
marked protein. The other protein is a decoy. Water escorts help the ion.

All rooms start with only two lives.
Room 1: pressure cage, 12 seconds, Protein A; a narrow 64-unit cage opening,
       two moving traps, and a 0.9-second hold inside an 18-unit protein pocket.
Room 2: decoy corridor, 15 seconds, Protein B; three moving traps and a
       0.8-second hold inside a 19-unit protein pocket.
Room 3: final lock, 12 seconds, Protein B; strong moving traps, narrow gates,
       and a moving protein target. Hold inside its 18-unit pocket for 1 second.
       The two barriers open for only 0.6 seconds in each 4.4-second cycle.
Docking requires remaining inside the pocket at a speed below 60 game units/s.
The hold resets if you leave the pocket or move too fast. Time a burst to
cross a briefly open barrier, then brake before docking.

Collect water: +50 points and a temporary shield. A shield reduces trap pull,
prevents trap damage, and lets the ion pass through an active barrier.
Shields last 1.8 seconds in room 1, 2 seconds in room 2, and only 1.2 seconds
in the final room.
They do not protect against choosing the wrong protein or touching an ignition core.
Reach the correct protein: +300 points.
Finish without damage: +100 points.
Each whole second left: +2 points.
Hit toxic rain, an active trap, barrier, or wrong protein: -40 points and -1 stability.
Two hits or an expired timer: GAME OVER, followed by "Try it later!"
One second of hit immunity prevents multiple hits from one instant contact.
Retry resets only the current room's score and time; previous room scores stay.
Fast-rain best scores are stored separately from earlier game versions,
only in the browser where local storage is available.

Move: arrows or WASD; hold a point in the room to steer toward it.
Burst: Space or the Burst button, with a 4-second recharge.
Pause: P or Pause. Retry: R or Retry. Touch controls are provided.
Backgrounding the page pauses gameplay. Sound is off until enabled.
Reduced-motion preferences reduce decorative animation without changing rules.

SCIENTIFIC SCOPE

This is an arcade game with explicit positive and negative reinforcement of
player choices through a fixed score system. It uses inertia, drag, collision
responses, and attractive trap forces in illustrative units. Water shielding,
barriers, target proteins, and docking scores are game mechanics; they are not
quantitative hydration, binding, molecular dynamics, or force-field results.
Arsenic is a metalloid, so As* denotes arsenic-containing species rather
than a free aqueous As3+ ion. Dissolved K+ is not potassium metal. The falling
particles and ignition cores are fictional game rules; this game is not a
chemistry safety guide, and water does not make toxic metals safe.
It has no external game dependencies, analytics, login, or server component.
The profile hits badge and statistics cards still use external services.
