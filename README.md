## 🔬 What I Actually Do (a.k.a. Research Interests)

- 🎗️ **Chemotherapy-Enhanced Cancer Treatment**  
  Using molecular modeling and data-driven design to outsmart chemoresistant cancer cells (they fight back, but so do we).

- 🧬 **Molecular Dynamics in Oncology**  
  Tracking conformational gymnastics, drug resistance, and protein–ligand drama at atomic resolution.

- ⚗️ **Peptide Inhibitor Design with ML + QM**  
  Teaching machines some quantum chemistry so they can help design smarter peptide inhibitors.

- 🧪 **Non-Equilibrium Free Energy Calculations**  
  Calculating free energy landscapes… because equilibrium is overrated.
