The chemistry behind the characters Explore the science ↗

Crimson. Golden yellow. Lilac.
Li, Na, and K give these familiar flame-test colours. The characters use screen approximations of those colours, not exact emission spectra or the colour of aqueous ions. The toxic characters use fantasy palettes.

A water entourage, oxygen first.
The oxygen end of each water molecule points toward the positive player ion. Li⁺ binds water more strongly than Na⁺ and K⁺. Longer shields and movement speeds translate that trend into playful trade-offs; the drawings do not specify coordination numbers.
